002 — Product architecture

Three proprietary platforms. One material system.

At the core of DSC are three material platforms — combined into high-performance packages engineered around each product's needs.

Open-cell · Comfort

DREAMCELL®

Comfort-driven, open-cell foam engineered for breathability and a soft, adaptive feel — built for all-day wearability and recovery, from morning to night.

Breathable Adaptive feel
Closed-cell · Performance

DPX®

Durapontex®

High-performance, responsive closed-cell foam built for energy return and durability — engineered with advanced processes like supercritical fluid foaming for consistent, resilient performance.

Energy return Durable
Adaptable · More than foam

MORPH™

Our customizable materials platform — combining DREAMCELL® and DPX® with engineered constructions. Delivered as molded components, sheets, or fully integrated material systems.
